Welcome — I'm 7im3Crisys, and I've been in automotive cybersecurity since 2015. I've watched this industry evolve from niche research into a critical discipline, and I've done my best to keep up with the latest tools, research, talks, and trainings along the way. This is the resource I wish existed when I was coming up — a curated learning path to help you gain expertise and find what interests you most. Built with contributions from noteworthy community professionals.
